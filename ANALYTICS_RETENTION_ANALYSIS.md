## 🎯 Root Causes of Poor Retention

### 1. **No Email Capture Strategy**
**Problem:** Users visit once and leave with no way to re-engage.

**Evidence:** 
- No newsletter signup visible
- No downloadable resources
- No content upgrades

**Solution:** 
- Add email newsletter signup with lead magnet
- Create downloadable PDF catalog
- Offer "Export Guide" or "Product Specifications" in exchange for email

### 2. **Weak Value Proposition on First Visit**
**Problem:** Users don't immediately understand value/trust.

**Evidence:**
- May not see clear differentiation
- No social proof prominently displayed
- No immediate call-to-action for engagement

**Solution:**
- Add testimonials/reviews section prominently
- Show case studies or success stories
- Add trust badges (certifications, years in business)
- Create engaging hero section with clear value prop

### 3. **No Content Marketing**
**Problem:** No reason to return - no fresh content, no blog.

**Evidence:**
- No blog section visible
- No educational content
- No industry insights or updates

**Solution:**
- Start a blog with weekly articles:
  - "Export Trends 2026"
  - "How to Import from India: Complete Guide"
  - "Product Spotlights" (feature products)
  - "Industry News & Updates"

### 4. **Limited Engagement Opportunities**
**Problem:** Users can only browse products or contact - no middle engagement.

**Evidence:**
- Only two main actions: browse catalog or contact
- No interactive elements
- No quizzes, calculators, or tools

**Solution:**
- Add "Get Quote" quick form (less commitment than full contact)
- Create "Product Finder" tool
- Add "Request Sample" option
- Implement chat widget for instant engagement

### 5. **No Social Media Integration**
**Problem:** Can't follow company for updates.

**Evidence:**
- No social media links prominent
- No sharing buttons on products
- No social feed on website

**Solution:**
- Add social media links in header/footer
- Add social sharing buttons on products
- Create LinkedIn company page
- Share regular updates on social platforms
